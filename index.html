Engagement

Right-sized monthly leadership

Same packaging language as Security Medic. Calm hours bands — scope grows with accountability, not with product upsells.

Starter

8–12 hrs / month

Discovery, essential policies, insurance readiness, and a 90-day roadmap.

  • Foundational Govern footprint
  • Good first engagement or renewal sprint

Standard

15–20 hrs / month

Adds IR playbook, awareness program, KPI dashboard, and vendor risk framework.

  • Ongoing fractional leadership
  • Typical fit for exam windows & MSP overlays

Comprehensive

25–35 hrs / month

Full executive leadership: board reporting depth, exam / SOC 2 readiness, and on-call IR leadership.

  • Active exam or board pressure
  • Highest accountability band
